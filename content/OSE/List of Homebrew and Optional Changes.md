This page details the Homebrew as well as the officially provided optional changes that Jim likes to use for Old School Essentials.

1. During character creation, you can swap two stats, up to two times. For example, you roll an 8 for STR, a 15 for INT, a 10 for WIS, a 14 for  DEX, an 8 CON, and a 12 for CHA. That is an array of 8 15 10 14 8 12. You can swap the INT/STR and the CHA/CON to get a final array of 15 8 10 14 12 8. You could swap just one stat pair, or you could leave it completely as you rolled it. 
2. ReRoll 1s and 2s for HP at level 1, and Secondary Skills (Step 13 of basic character creation method).
3. Roll to determine if you can choose a rare class (see [[Character Creation]]). All of the classes in Carcass Crawler 1 through 5 are allowed, but the classes may be broken and may require slight adjustments for fairness to all players.
4. Equipment from Carcass Crawler 3 is allowed.
