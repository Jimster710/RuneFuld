> The mystical lands of RuneFuld-our new continent, and a hope of escape from the blight.

## The Blight drives us onward...

Two decades of crop blight has destabilized all lands of the sphere. Desperation has pushed many to make perilous treks through the Underdark, or across monster ridden seas. The new continent of RuneFuld was thus discovered-pilgrims, refugees, survivors, kings-all scrambled to establish land holdings. 

Despite best efforts-the blight has spread to the new continent. Strict controls have stifled trade. Rumours of blight are enough to burn villages and entire fields. 

The source of the blight has been subject to years of study. A combination of natural and magical effects are wrapped into this insidious disease. The people are quick to blame the creatures of the wilds and the Dungeon-but no one is certain.
