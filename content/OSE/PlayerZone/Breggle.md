This is a class developed from OSE's related setting, Dolmenwood. The original inspiration can be found [here](https://www.dolmenwood.necroticgnome.com/rules/doku.php?id=breggle_kindred-class).

- **Requirements:** Strength and Charisma of at least 9. At least a 16 in one of the prime abilities, and at least a 13 in the other.
- **Prime Abilities:** Dexterity and Constitution
    
- **Hit Points:** 1d6 per Level, +2 after Level 10
    
- **Combat Aptitude:** Martial
    
- **Armour:** Leather, Chainmail & Shields
    
- **Weapons:** Any, except Longbows and 2-Handed Weapons
    
- **Native Languages:** Common and the Secret Language of Goats

Breggle adventurers are capable combatants, wielding both weapons of steel and their natural horns with precision. At higher Levels, they also gain magical aptitude, including the ability to cast arcane spells.


## Fur

A breggle character’s thick, woolly fur grants them +1 AC when unarmoured or wearing Light armour. The bonus is not granted when wearing Chainmail or other Medium Armor.

## Horns

Breggles may make a melee attack with their horns instead of a weapon. The damage inflicted increases with Level, as indicated in the Breggle Horns table. The horns can also trigger a charge attack (see the description in the weapons/equipment section of the Player's Tome)

### Horn Length and Social Standing

A Breggle character begins play as a commoner of the shorthorn caste aspiring to improve their lot in life. As they advance in Level, gaining experience, wealth, and renown, their horns grow (see the Breggle Horns table).

### Longhorn Status

Upon reaching Level 4, a Breggle character’s horns have grown to the point that the character is recognized as a longhorn.

## Breggle Skills
▶ Climb sheer surfaces (CS): A roll is
required for each 100’ to be climbed. If
the roll fails, the Breggle falls at the halfway
point, suffering falling damage.
▶ Tightrope walking (TW): Breggles
can walk along tightropes, narrow beams,
and ledges at up to half their normal
movement rate. A roll is required every
60’. Failure indicates that the Breggle falls
and suffers falling damage. Windy con-
ditions may reduce the chance of success
by up to 20%. Holding a balance pole
increases the chance of success by 10%.

## Druidic Magic

Upon attaining longhorn status (from Level 4), Breggles begin to study nature magic.

**Spell casting:** The Breggle Spells Per Day table shows the number of spells a Breggle may memorize, determined by the character’s Level. For example, a Level 6 Breggle may memorize at most two Rank 1 spells and one Rank 2 spell.

**Learning spells:** A longhorn does not automatically know any spells and must learn spells from a mentor, from discovered spell books, or by research.

**Using magic items:** Breggles of Level 4 or higher can use magic items that may only be used by Druidic spell casters.
