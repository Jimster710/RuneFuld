1d20 connections or obligations

1. You have a partner, and maybe children in a nearby town. A portion of your income (10%) must go to supporting them -or face the consequences. You and your party never gain max heat in that town and can rest there for free.
2. You are an orphan, and you need to strike a deal with a safe town to move the orphanage to. You feel morally obligated to do so, but are under no contract.
3. You used to be a squire! Any keep or castle will have a better reaction to you, but only if you maintain chivalric obligations to any knights, paladins or clerics you meet.
4. You are somewhat of a local legend, and have chance of be recognized. Unfortunately, an adoring fan insists upon following you everywhere.
5. You used to be a farmer, but the blight drove you to this land. You owe a Feudal Lord a significant sum, and they will send people to hunt you down.
6. You used to be a scribe, and have no problems finding sages or where to research- but you owe your institution a significant work, treasure, or discovery.
7. You are a known criminal, and guards will always be suspicious of you, On the Flipside it is easy for you to find a person to fence stolen goods.
8. You are a gambler! You either have a small Fortune and people who hate you For it, or you have a debt of 2d6x 500gp and some gambling buddies.
9. You're coming back out of Retirement to adventure, Roll 1d4! 1, You're broke, but have a magic item. 2. You get a pension of 200gp per month, but you were supposed to pay taxes on it. 3. You are missing a limb, but towns will always give you a mule if they can spare one. 4. Your wisdom is maxed, but your strength or dexterity must go to 3.
10. You're a wild person, and make townsfolk uncomfortable, Heat level in town can never be baseline, but you can Forage for 1d6 daily iron rations per day in the woods.
11. You are an escapee from a cult. Take 5 points From WIS and move it to NT, which cannot be increased above 18, Any extra pants are not lost from WIS.
12. You were decently high up in a local religion, you gain services at temples easily, but you must give a small donation to any worthy cause you find.
13. You are a well to-do explorer, basically on tour/expedition to be exposed to the real world, Max starting wealth, but you Must take -3 From some combination OF CON Or CHA.
14. You have a progressive illness, You are either seeking a Cure CON Set to 3, or you are making your last days count (WIS 18, but you don't have long).
15. You were an exchange student. Get +1 language. But your race or the exchange race tend to react to you negatively.
16. You were a merchant. Hiring retainers is easier for you, but you always get charged extra for tolls and fees.
17. You were a cook but you are very picky with food. Any Foraged or hunted Food you have a 50% chance of refusing. However, your meals heal 1 HP Per day in Camp.
18. You are an absolute Flirt, and always make a romance save for every town trip. If you fail, heat will increase.
19. You are an active preacher or missionary. Your reaction rolls In town, other than at temples are worse, however you have a bonus of +2 to CHA (not to be increased above 18).
20. The social climber, You must spend at least half of your income in town for each visit on the latest fashions, but heat can never increase to maximum, and many Commoners (or even retainers) will think you are someone important.
