This page details the Homebrew as well as the officially provided optional changes that Jim likes to use for Old School Essentials.

## Changes to Specific Classes

All Martial classes (Fighter, Dwarf, etc.) get a save vs. death upon being reduced to zero HP. This can only be used once per day.

- Fighter
	- Fighters get two Maneuvers (Combat Talents) which you can roll for randomly. They are found in Carcass Crawler 1 (see [[Character Creation]] for the drive link).

- Thief (and Assassin)
	- Thieves (and Assassins) can backstab/assassinate in combat. You must approach the enemy from behind and either succeed on a Move Silently roll or the target must be otherwise distracted. An enemy cannot be surprised in this way twice, and it may be more difficult to repeat this against other enemies that witness it. Certain enemies cannot be surprised in this way.
	- Thieves auto succeed on certain skill checks. (For example, if they can climb completely sheer surfaces on a roll, that means they can climb something like a tree without a roll at all.)

- Magic-User
	- Magic-Users get random cantrips from the rules in Carcass Crawler 5. The optional read magic cantrip is not allowed.
	- Magic-Users gain access to all spells for the level at which they can cast. They have to choose which to prepare, but they immediately have access to all of the spells.
- Necromancer
	- Necromancers get random cantrips (2 at level 1) from the rules in Carcass Crawler 5. The optional read magic cantrip is not allowed.


## General Changes
1. During character creation, you can swap two stats, up to two times. For example, you roll an 8 for STR, a 15 for INT, a 10 for WIS, a 14 for  DEX, an 8 CON, and a 12 for CHA. That is an array of 8 15 10 14 8 12. You can swap the INT/STR and the CHA/CON to get a final array of 15 8 10 14 12 8. You could swap just one stat pair, or you could leave it completely as you rolled it. 
2. Max HP at Level 1 (+ or - CON mod), and Secondary Skills (Step 13 of basic character creation method).
3. Roll to determine if you can choose a rare class (see [[Character Creation]]). All of the classes in Carcass Crawler 1 through 5 are allowed, but the classes may be broken and may require slight adjustments for fairness to all players.
4. Equipment from Carcass Crawler 3 is encouraged.
5. [[Slot based encumbrance ]]rules. No need to track water consumption outside of desert areas. Rations will be tracked.
