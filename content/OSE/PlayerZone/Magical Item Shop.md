You can sell your magic items in town! Each time town is visited and magic items are available, consult this area-

2d6 magic items are known to be in the possession of someone in town. 

Each Price is in the range of +-24% of an unknown base cost-but magic items are generally not bought and sold in a marketplace. These markups/markdowns may refer to gold, but they could also refer to the type of favor or trade you need to provide to get the item!
