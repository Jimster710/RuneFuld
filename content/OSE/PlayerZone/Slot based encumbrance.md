This will make tracking gear/weight much much easier and faster than counting pounds/coins. It isn't representative of bulk, volume, or weight in any realistic way. It's just a way to game-ify the system without throwing it out or tracking tiny details.

Every character starts with 10 slots, but that is adjusted by twice your CON modifier. 

> A CON score of 8 is a modifier of -1. Twice that modifier is -2, and thus you'd only have 8 slots. 
> 
> A CON score of 13 is a modifier of +1. Twice that modifier is +2, and so you'd have 12 slots.
