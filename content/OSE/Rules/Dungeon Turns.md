Dungeon Turns take 10 minutes on in-game time. Each turn is tracked, and brings you closer to a torch or lantern running out-or a wandering monster appearing. 

Major activities in the dungeon will often take a turn-sometimes several turns. This is the way that OSE tracks the stress and resources required to dungeon delve.

Notice that a wandering monster check occurs every two turns, and a rest (R) is required once within every six turns.
