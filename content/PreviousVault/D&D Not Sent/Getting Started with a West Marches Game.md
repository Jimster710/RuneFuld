## Brave Adventurers Welcome

You are about to embark upon epic quests, earn loot, fight monsters, and gain fame and fortune in the West Marches!

**First, some explanation-**
A West Marches Dungeons and Dragons game is a style of play that involves a group of adventurers journeying into the unsettled wilderness. There may be a border town nearby, but out west is where the real magic happens! You undertake adventures for fame, magic items, XP, and most importantly, for gold!

Here's how it works-a player or group of players want to journey to a specific hex on the map, a game session is organized, and a session is ran. Your character may live or die out there in the wilderness, but at the end of the session-everyone returns to the border town ready for the next adventure. This makes it super easy to drop in and drop out even if your schedule changes on the fly, because you only play in games you can make it to! If a time doesn't work for you, you could suggest another time in session planning and see if a DM can run it for you. There are no multi-session arcs, although there can be multi-session dungeons. 

**Ready to start? Here's what you'll need!**

Access to the Discord server-https://discord.gg/zFSjGGKR2n

Everyone will always have three active characters. It's your choice as to their classes and abilities, so build them as normal. Every new character starts at level 3, but if they survive, they'll get to keep those sweet bits of gear and XP. 

If you lose a character during an adventure, you can swap in one of your other characters on the fly. 


In person sessions grant a 10% XP bonus. Hurray for social interaction!

DMs get 1/2 of the XP for any game that they run. You didn't even have to risk a character and you still get a reward, look at that! (Even more reason to go out and DM some games)


**Regarding the map...**
The farther west you go, the more dangerous the enemies become, but the greater the rewards. Some zones have level minimums. At least half of the party must be at or above the level minimum to venture there. 

**Gold is actually useful!**

Every week, a list of magic items will be generated in the town. These, along with all standard/common items, can be purchased in the town.

Travelling through hexes has a gold cost per hex, which can be reduced through several means. 

Knowledge of fruitful hexes and dungeon locations are extremely valuable, and you can use your gold to purchase these things from the three guilds.

Level 5 and up characters have Bastions! These things give you bonuses, but you'll need gold to fuel them!

**Regarding travel**
Each hex you travel through has a chance of a random encounter. The encounters can be good or bad.

Eventually, as the wilderness is conquered, roads and paths will be built which will eliminate these risks-but it will push the good loot ever further west...

When you arrive at your destination, you'll take some time to scout around if you do not have specific knowledge of a dungeon or other point of interest in the area. Finding things out in the wilderness is quite valuable to the guilds!

**Regarding the Guilds**
There are three guilds in the West Marches, the Venture Guild, the Circle, and the Domain. 

Membership in these guilds comes with benefits, but must be earned! Membership is open to any class, but each guild has a goal in the Marches. Help each guild with their goal, and membership will be yours.

You can only belong to one guild at a time.

*The Circle*
The Circle is a collection of Druids, Barbarians, Monks, and Sorcerers. Their goal is to avoid disturbing the natural order of forces and balance in the Marches, but they don't mind as much if powerful magical items happen to fall into their hands. They love the forces of the Fey, and they despise Constructs and Aberrations. 

*The Venture Guild*
The Venture Guild are a collection of Rangers, Rogues, Warlocks, and Fighters. Their goal is to settle the marches, but in reality it is to find as much treasure as possible. They love any monster that gives them money, and they despise Dragons.

*The Domain*
The Domain is a collection of Clerics, Paladins, Bards, and Wizards. Their goal is to civilize, find holy relics and sites, and cleanse the marches of evil. They love Celestial forces, and they despise the Undead.
