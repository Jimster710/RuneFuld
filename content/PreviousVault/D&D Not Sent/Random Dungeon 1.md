Area surrounding dungeon is a wastes or wasteland. Nothing growing, acidic pools, mangled trees, and loose barren soils. This is clearly a corrupted place.

Partly cloudly weather. 10% chance of earthquakes for each day spent.

Monsters/Factions-
Orcs, Bandits, Cockatrice Regent

1. Stairs leading down is the entryway. The stench of death is present. Crumbled stone can be seen strewn about the entryway, except for a small amount that appears to be cleared away. 
2. Dining room, with rotting food, golden goblets, and rats crawling out of the way. (The water in the goblets is a strong poison, con save for half damage. Moving the goblet at the head of the table triggers all the goblets to start filling the room with water)
3. A door and hallway leads to the right, ending in a room with 4 doors. 
	1. Orcish forge (Has Orcs)
	2. Common bandit room (cards, tables, etc.) (Has Bandits)
	3. A "Great Hall" down the last path
		1. The Orcish forge and Common Bandit room are connected, and are fighting. Gold coin treasure no matter who sided with. Orc Forge gives 1d4 Orc Armor Upgrade (temporary spikes). Bandit Den gives extra 2d12 x 50gp
4. Nest-Regular Cockatrice.
5. Cockatrice Regent. The great hall has mundane items loot.
