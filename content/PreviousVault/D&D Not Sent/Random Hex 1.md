Clear Skies, but with an 86% chance of severe thunderstorm.

This is a desert area, magical item loot, fey or plant enemy, warlock, barbarian, aasimar. Spike trap.

A shrine can be seen across the dunes, the red stones stretch out towards the searing firey sun. **Upon Approach, an Aasimar will appear, asking if the travelers are worthy, and asking for an offering before an altar.** 

A warlock, Eldarion, and his barbarian partner, Ulafaren are in this desert in search of a mystical oasis, built by the desert elves. Ulafaren has traveled there before, and can find his way with a mystical compass. He brought Eldarion along to gain entry into this oasis, but Eldarion could not break the magic seal. 

The oasis- travelling through burnt dunes, you eventually find yourself at a towering wall of sand, the largest dune you have seen. Ulafaren states that this pillar extends all the way around the area. Attempting to climb the sand results in falling backwards. Birds can be seen flying overhead, you must be close to water. Ulafaren will lead you around this path, until you gaze upon a sandstone door. In the center of the door, an Ivory inlay can be observed. (Show Image)

Inside the Oasis, if it is daytime, everything is quite nice. Water, abundant plant life, the cooling mist of a waterfall. The only thing that seems unusual is the occasional elf corpse. 

At night however, the Myconids will emerge. The corpses will animate as spore servants.
