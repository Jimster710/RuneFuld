Traveller uses a lifepath system for character creation, and in general it is a skills-heavy game. 

The basic roll is a 2d6 roll, add the results, and on a 7 or higher, you succeed. 2s and 12s being special critical failures/successes is an optional rule. A result of 6 can also be a success, but with a complication or a requirement to sacrifice something. 

The actual determination of which skill to apply is mostly up to the players to pitch to the Referee. 

Ground combat is deadly. Space combat is like submarine warfare. This is an extremely crunchy game-but the role-play elements are very easy.
